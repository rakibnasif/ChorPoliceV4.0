Babu Dakat

Babu hukum dey, Police chhute, Chor-Dakat mukh lukay. Kemon kore khelbe?

How to play

Game e 4 jon player, ar 4 ta card. Protita card e ekta role ar point ache:

Babu100
Police80
Dakat60
Chor40

Ek round er niyom

  1. Sobai ekta kore card pay. Nijer card ta chepe dhore dekho, chere dile lukiye jabe. Onno keu jeno na dekhe!
  2. Sobai dekhe nile Babu ar Police er card sobar shamne khule jay. Dakat ar Chor ke, ta shudhu tara nijera jane.
  3. Babu Police ke hukum dey: "Chor khojo" ba "Dakat khojo".
  4. Police baki 2 ta lukano card er moddhe ekta chape. Thik hole: dhora pore gelo, oi Chor/Dakat pay 0. Bhul hole: Police pay 0, ar Chor/Dakat nijer point rakhe.
  5. Baki sobai nijer role er point pay. Score table e round er point jog hote thake. Summary e dekho kar rank Raja, Minister, Proja, ar Slave.

Mode onujayi

Play Locally: naam likhe Start dao. Protita player card dekhe "Done, pass the phone" chepe phone porer jon ke dibe. Card dekhar shomoy onno keu jeno na dekhe.
Multiplayer: ekjon Room banao chape, 4 okkhor er code bondhuder pathay. Tara code diye join kore. 4 jon na ashle baki seat e bot khelbe. Net chole gele seat ta thake, ferot ashle abar game e dhukte pare.

Tip: Chor ba Dakat hole poker face rakho, ar Police hole mukh dekhe bujhe nao!
